Order of the weekend

Friday, Sep 17 | 18:30

Courtyard Aperitifs

Local rose, wood-fired flatbreads, and acoustic guitar in the lower olive terrace. Casual summer tailoring.

Saturday, Sep 18 | 16:00

Ceremony & Dinner

Vows in the limestone chapel followed by champagne in the orchard and a four-course long-table supper.

Sunday, Sep 19 | 11:30

Farewell Garden Lunch

Fresh pastries, espresso, and Provencal salads beside the pool before afternoon departures to Marseille.

Travel & stay notes

Getting there Marseille Provence Airport (MRS) is 35 minutes by car; Aix-en-Provence TGV station is 22 minutes away.
Room blocks Preferred guest rates are held at Hotel Le Pigonnet and Villa Saint-Ange until July 1, 2027.
Evening shuttles Private coaches run between central Aix and the estate at 15:15, 23:30, and 01:15.
